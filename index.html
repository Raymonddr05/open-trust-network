How many times have you been lied to, cheated, used, or hurt on purpose?

Almost everyone has experienced moral evil, and too often it is rewarded.

AI and robots will likely run most of our systems in the near future. If moral evil is still rewarded then, they will do it faster and at a much larger scale than humans can, and we may not be able to stop them. Unless we do something now.

The vision is a world where everyone is kept safe, left free, treated fairly, and helped to grow, belong, and contribute by one another. In that world, moral good is the norm, and moral evil is unprofitable, unviable, and rare.

The mission is to build an open trust network of people, businesses, AIs, and robots where moral good is rewarded and moral evil is not.

The network starts with a small group who wants to deal in moral good. As the network grows, it will be openly reported how members are doing compared with non-members. When members are better off in terms of their quality of life, more non-members would likely want to join. Growth is expected to be very slow at the start, then have a snowball effect as it scales worldwide.

Join me, and let’s get this right.
