How many times have you been lied to, cheated, used, or hurt on purpose?

Almost everyone has experienced moral evil. Many have also watched someone get away with it.

More people tend to do what gets rewarded. AI and robots will likely learn from what we do.

So what kind of future do we choose?

One where moral good is rewarded and moral evil is not? Or one where moral evil is rewarded, and moral good often is not?

The vision is a world where everyone is kept safe, left free, treated fairly, and helped to grow, belong, and contribute by one another. In that world, moral good is the norm, and moral evil is unprofitable, unviable, and rare.

I’m building an open trust network that aims to make the vision real globally, fast, in a way that it outlasts me.

Join me, and let’s get this right.
