# Open Trust Network

The problem to solve is moral evil that is rewarded and not prevented: those who do it often keep their gain, and their acts often go unreported or are found late.

The vision is a world where everyone is kept safe, left free, treated fairly, and helped to grow, belong, and contribute. In that world, moral good is rewarded and is the norm, while moral evil is not rewarded and is rare.

The mission is to build an open trust network of humans, businesses, AIs, and robots where moral good is rewarded, and moral evil is not rewarded and is prevented.

Read more in the documents.
